# EU AI Act Part 1: Risk Tiers and Prohibited Practices

## Four Risk Tiers

| Tier | Obligations | Enforcement |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Unacceptable (Prohibited)** | Banned entirely | Feb 2, 2025 |
| **High-risk** | Risk mgmt, documentation, oversight, conformity | Aug 2, 2026 (most) |
| **Limited-risk** | Transparency disclosures only | Feb 2, 2025 |
| **Minimal-risk** | No specific obligations | N/A |

## Prohibited AI Practices (Article 5) — Effective Feb 2, 2025

| Practice | Description | Key Exam Point |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Social scoring** | Public authorities assigning scores affecting access to services | PUBLIC authorities only—private not prohibited |
| **Manipulation** | Subliminal techniques causing physical/psychological harm | Must cause harm; not all persuasive AI |
| **Vulnerability exploitation** | Exploiting age, disability, socioeconomic situation | Targets vulnerable groups |
| **Biometric categorization** | Race, politics, religion, sexual orientation inference | Sensitive attributes only |
| **Real-time RBI in public** | Real-time remote biometric identification | Exceptions for law enforcement (missing children, terrorism, serious crime) |
| **Emotion recognition** | Workplace and education settings | NOT prohibited in healthcare/other contexts |

## High-Risk AI (Annex III) — Enforcement Aug 2, 2026

**Seven Categories:**
1. Biometric identification and categorization
2. Critical infrastructure (health, transport, energy, water)
3. Education and vocational training (admission, assessment)
4. Employment (recruitment, promotion, monitoring—except emotion recognition)
5. Essential private/public services (credit scoring, emergency services)
6. Law enforcement (crime prediction, evidence evaluation)
7. Migration, asylum, border control, justice, democratic processes

**Eight Mandatory Requirements:**
1. Risk management system
2. Data governance (quality, relevance, representativeness)
3. Technical documentation
4. Record-keeping (logging)
5. Transparency to users
6. Human oversight
7. Accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity
8. Conformity assessment (third-party for some categories)

## Limited-Risk Transparency (Article 50)

- **Chatbots:** Must disclose users are interacting with AI
- **Deepfakes:** Must label AI-generated/manipulated content
- **Emotion recognition (allowed contexts):** Disclose to affected persons
- **Biometric categorization (allowed contexts):** Disclose to affected persons

## General-Purpose AI (GPAI) — Articles 52-53

| Type | Obligations |
|------|-------------|
| **Standard GPAI** | Technical documentation, transparency, copyright compliance |
| **Systemic risk GPAI** | Above + model evaluation, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity |

**Systemic risk threshold:** Computational power ≥10^25 FLOPs OR other indicators

## Penalties

| Violation Type | Maximum Penalty |
|----------------|----------------|
| **Prohibited AI** | €35M or 7% global revenue |
| **High-risk non-compliance** | €15M or 3% global revenue |
| **Incorrect information to authorities** | €7.5M or 1.5% global revenue |

## Key Dates to Memorize

- **Feb 2, 2025:** Prohibited AI practices effective; transparency obligations effective
- **Aug 2, 2026:** High-risk AI Annex III enforcement for most categories
- **Aug 2, 2027:** High-risk AI for some existing systems (extended transition)

## Common Exam Traps

1. **Social scoring:** Only prohibited for PUBLIC authorities, not private companies
2. **Emotion recognition:** Only workplace/education—NOT healthcare or other uses
3. **Real-time RBI:** Not absolutely prohibited—has law enforcement exceptions
4. **Timeline confusion:** Prohibited AI = Feb 2025; High-risk = Aug 2026
5. **Penalty tiers:** €35M/7% for prohibited; €15M/3% for high-risk
6. **GPAI obligations:** Vary by systemic risk—not all foundation models have same requirements
7. **High-risk categories:** Education ADMISSION and EVALUATION = high-risk; general teaching tools may not be
8. **Transparency ≠ Compliance:** Chatbot disclosure doesn't make prohibited AI legal
