## Chapter 10: Full AIGP Exam Simulation and Test Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | AIGP exam format: How many questions, how much time, what passing score? | 100 multiple-choice questions, 150 minutes (90 seconds per question average), scaled scoring with no published pass score (estimated ~70% correct needed). Exam tip: Time management is critical—flag questions taking >2 minutes. |
| 2 | AIGP exam domain distribution: How many questions per domain (approximate)? | Domain 5 (Laws): ~27 questions (27%), Domain 4 (Risk/Governance): ~25 questions (25%), Domain 3 (Responsible AI): ~20 questions (20%), Domain 2 (Lifecycle): ~15 questions (15%), Domain 1 (Foundations): ~13 questions (13%). Exam tip: Expect most questions from Domains 4-5. |
| 3 | AIGP question type distribution? | 60% scenario-based (apply concept to situation), 30% direct knowledge (which article, which definition), 10% comparison (X vs Y). Exam tip: Scenario questions test application, not memorization—identify the constraint first. |
| 4 | Flag-and-return strategy: When to flag a question? | Flag any question taking >2 minutes (exceeds 90-second average). Return after completing easier questions. Exam tip: Never spend 4+ minutes on one question—all questions worth same points. |
| 5 | How to read AIGP scenario questions? | Identify three things: (1) the key constraint (jurisdiction, risk tier, lifecycle stage), (2) the governance challenge (compliance, risk mitigation, transparency), (3) the compliance requirement (which law/framework applies). Exam tip: Constraint is usually in first 1-2 sentences. |
| 6 | Eliminating wrong answers: What are the five common flaws in wrong answer choices? | Too broad (covers more than question asks), too narrow (misses key requirement), wrong jurisdiction (applies law from wrong region), wrong risk tier (confuses prohibited/high-risk/limited-risk), wrong lifecycle stage (confuses design vs deployment obligations). Exam tip: Eliminate answers with ANY of these flaws. |
| 7 | Common trap: GDPR Article 22 vs EU AI Act Article 14? | GDPR Article 22: solely automated decision-making with legal/significant effects—requires human intervention. EU AI Act Article 14: human oversight for HIGH-RISK AI systems—requires human-in-the-loop or on-the-loop. Exam tip: Article 22 applies to specific decisions; Article 14 applies to high-risk AI system design. |
| 8 | Common trap: Provider vs Deployer obligations? | Provider (EU AI Act): develops/supplies AI system—obligations include conformity assessment, technical documentation, CE marking. Deployer: puts AI system into service—obligations include human oversight, monitoring, incident reporting. Exam tip: 'Who does X' questions test this distinction. |
| 9 | Common trap: NIST GOVERN vs MANAGE function? | GOVERN: establishes policies, culture, accountability (governance structure). MANAGE: ongoing monitoring, response, improvement (operational activities). Exam tip: 'monitoring deployed systems' = MANAGE, 'establishing oversight policies' = GOVERN. |
| 10 | Domain 5 (Laws) strategy? | Memorize article numbers: GDPR Article 22 (automated decisions), Article 35 (DPIA), EU AI Act Article 5 (prohibited AI), Article 6-7 (high-risk classification), Article 14 (human oversight). Memorize risk tiers: prohibited vs high-risk vs limited-risk. Exam tip: ~27 questions on Domain 5—most require article recall. |
| 11 | Domain 3 (Responsible AI) strategy? | Focus on scenario-based bias identification: protected characteristics (race, gender, age), bias in training data vs model outputs, fairness metrics (demographic parity, equalized odds), mitigation techniques. Exam tip: Questions describe a scenario and ask 'what type of bias' or 'how to mitigate'. |
| 12 | Time management checkpoint: 50 questions in how many minutes to stay on pace? | 75 minutes (50 questions × 90 seconds average = 75 minutes). Exam tip: Check your pace at 25 questions (37.5 min), 50 questions (75 min), 75 questions (112.5 min). If behind pace, flag more questions. |
