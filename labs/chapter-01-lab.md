# Chapter 1 Lab: Foundations of AI Systems for Governance

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Classify three real AI systems (chatbot, credit scoring, medical diagnostic) by architecture type and identify the provider, deployer, and any importers under EU AI Act definitions. Document in an AI inventory template.

## Key takeaway

The exam tests AI system components and lifecycle actor roles (provider/deployer/importer) more than technical AI architectures — focus on governance accountability, not algorithms.
