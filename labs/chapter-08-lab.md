# Chapter 8 Lab: EU AI Act Part 1: Risk Tiers and Prohibited Practices

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Classify five AI systems under EU AI Act risk tiers: (1) employee wellness app analyzing facial emotions, (2) credit scoring for loans, (3) spam filter, (4) predictive policing tool, (5) chatbot for customer service. For each, state the risk tier, applicable articles, compliance obligations, and enforcement date. Create a compliance roadmap for the high-risk systems targeting August 2, 2026.

## Key takeaway

The exam heavily tests Article 5 prohibited AI by scenario (emotion recognition in workplace/education, real-time RBI in public, social scoring) and Annex III high-risk classification — memorize the eight high-risk categories and the August 2, 2026 compliance deadline for most high-risk AI.
