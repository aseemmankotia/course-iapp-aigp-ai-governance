# Chapter 10 Lab: Full AIGP Exam Simulation and Test Strategy

**Estimated time:** 180 minutes

## Exercise

Full Exam Simulation: 100 questions covering all five domains in domain-weight proportions. Take under timed conditions (150 minutes). After completion, review each question with detailed explanation of correct answer and why each distractor is wrong. Includes: (1) 27 Domain 5 questions (EU AI Act risk classification, conformity assessment, GDPR intersection, US laws), (2) 25 Domain 4 questions (NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, AI inventories, incident response), (3) 20 Domain 3 questions (bias scenarios, explainability, human oversight, PETs), (4) 15 Domain 2 questions (lifecycle artifacts, procurement, documentation), (5) 13 Domain 1 questions (AI types, foundation models, actor roles). Scoring breakdown by domain to identify weak areas.

## Key takeaway

The final exam simulation tests your ability to apply all domains under time pressure (90 seconds per question) — practice identifying question types (scenario vs direct knowledge), eliminating wrong answers by finding the flaw (wrong jurisdiction, wrong actor, wrong risk tier), and managing 150 minutes across 100 questions with domain-weight awareness (spend more time on Domain 5 questions since they are 27% of exam).
