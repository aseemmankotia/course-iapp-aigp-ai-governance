# Chapter 2 Lab: AI Development Lifecycle and Documentation

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Draft a model card for a resume-screening AI system. Include sections: intended use, training data demographics, known limitations, bias testing results, and monitoring plan. Then create a vendor due diligence checklist for procuring this system from a third party.

## Key takeaway

The exam heavily tests what documentation is legally required (EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation) vs best practices (model cards), and who is accountable for monitoring after deployment.
