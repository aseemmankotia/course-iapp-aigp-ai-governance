# Chapter 7 Lab: AI Incident Response, Audit, and Assurance

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Simulate an AI incident — a loan-approval AI denies 90% of applications from one postal code. Walk through incident response: (1) contain: pause system, (2) investigate: analyze data distribution and model behavior, (3) identify root cause: training data underrepresented that postal code, (4) remediate: retrain with balanced data, (5) document: incident report with timeline, (6) report: determine if regulatory notification required (GDPR breach? EU AI Act serious incident per Article 73?).

## Key takeaway

The exam tests when an AI failure becomes a reportable incident (EU AI Act Article 73: serious incidents with harm or fundamental rights violations; GDPR Article 33: personal data breaches) and distinguishes algorithmic audits (model testing) from process audits (governance compliance verification).
