# Chapter 5 Lab: NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

**Estimated time:** 22 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Map a facial recognition system used for workplace access control to NIST AI RMF. Complete a GOVERN worksheet (assign accountability roles), MAP worksheet (identify risks: bias, spoofing, privacy), MEASURE worksheet (define false acceptance rate threshold), MANAGE worksheet (monitoring plan, incident response for unauthorized access). Use NIST Playbook suggested actions.

## Key takeaway

The exam tests your ability to map scenarios to the four NIST AI RMF functions (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage) and identify which of the seven trustworthy characteristics applies to a given risk — memorize the function definitions and characteristics list.
