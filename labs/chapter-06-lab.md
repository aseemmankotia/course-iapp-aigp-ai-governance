# Chapter 6 Lab: ISO/IEC 42001 and AI Governance Programs

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Create an AI system inventory entry for a customer service chatbot. Include: system name, business owner, risk classification (EU AI Act: limited-risk transparency obligation), data sources, third-party components (LLM provider), human oversight mechanism, and monitoring metrics. Use ISO/IEC 42001 Annex A control mapping.

## Key takeaway

The exam tests ISO/IEC 42001 clause structure (especially Clause 6 planning and Annex A controls) and distinguishes internal AI inventories (governance best practice) from mandatory AI registries (EU AI Act Article 71 legal requirement for high-risk AI).
