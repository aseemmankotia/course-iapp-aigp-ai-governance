# Chapter 4 Lab: Privacy, Safety, and Societal Impacts of AI

**Estimated time:** 16 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: A hospital wants to train an AI on patient records without centralizing sensitive data across departments. Evaluate federated learning vs differential privacy vs synthetic data. Draft a one-page recommendation with pros, cons, and regulatory alignment (GDPR Article 25 data protection by design).

## Key takeaway

The exam tests PET selection by scenario (federated learning for decentralized training, synthetic data for sharing, differential privacy for aggregate queries) and requires recognizing when sector-specific laws (employment discrimination, fair lending) override general AI governance principles.
