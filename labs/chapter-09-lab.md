# Chapter 9 Lab: EU AI Act Part 2: High-Risk Compliance & Global AI Laws

**Estimated time:** 22 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: A company in Germany deploys high-risk AI for credit scoring. Create a compliance checklist mapping EU AI Act Articles 8-15 to specific actions: (1) Article 10 data governance — document training data sources, quality checks; (2) Article 11 technical documentation — prepare Annex IV file; (3) Article 12 logs — set up automated logging of decisions; (4) Article 14 human oversight — define override procedure; (5) Annex VI conformity assessment — complete internal assessment and CE marking. Then identify GDPR obligations: Article 35 DPIA for high-risk processing, Article 6 legal basis for using customer data.

## Key takeaway

The exam tests detailed knowledge of EU AI Act Articles 8-15 high-risk obligations (especially Articles 10, 13, 14), conformity assessment procedures (internal Annex VI vs notified body Annex VII), and the GDPR-AI Act intersection for AI using personal data — these three areas are heavily weighted in Domain 5.
