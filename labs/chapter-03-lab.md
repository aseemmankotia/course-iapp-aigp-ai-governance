# Chapter 3 Lab: Responsible AI: Fairness, Bias, and Transparency

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Lab: Analyze a hiring AI scenario where 80% of training resumes are male. Identify the bias type (representation bias). Propose three mitigation strategies: re-sample training data, use fairness constraints during training, or apply post-processing threshold adjustment. Map each to a stage in the AI lifecycle.

## Key takeaway

The exam tests specific bias types by scenario (measurement bias via proxies is heavily tested), and requires knowing which human oversight model (HITL vs HOTL) applies to which high-risk use case under EU AI Act Article 14.
